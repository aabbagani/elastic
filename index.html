About Elastic

The problem

Habit trackers assume steady energy. Partial effort counts as a miss, one bad day resets the streak, and people quit while they still care.

What this prototype does

  • Mini, Plus and Elite tiers, so a low-energy day still counts.
  • Fuel instead of a streak: it eases off when you miss a day and never resets.
  • Hide for today and Snooze, for days you can't control.
  • A focus timer and simulated browser tracking, so logging takes no extra step.
  • A Protocol Insight with COM-B scores. Here it runs on fixed rules, not AI.

What it leaves out

A real browser extension, accounts, social features and a mobile app. The data is made up and stays in this browser.
